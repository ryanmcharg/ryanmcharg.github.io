About

I create modern, emotionally-driven production with punch, depth, and clarity. My focus is helping artists sound competitive while keeping their identity intact.

All projects come complete with stems, free revisions, high-resolution WAV masters (24-bit / 44.1kHz, with options for 48kHz or 96kHz), and session files on request.
