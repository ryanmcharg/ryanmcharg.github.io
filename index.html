About

I create modern, emotionally-driven production with punch, depth, and clarity. My focus is helping artists sound competitive while keeping their identity intact.

I use Logic Pro X and plugins including FabFilter, Waves, iZotope, Melodyne and more. All projects include stems, free revisions, and high-resolution WAV masters (24-bit/44.1kHz or 48kHz/96kHz).

Do not hesitate to get in contact with all kinds of commissions, including but not limited to musical compositions, sync, lyric-writing, session-work, and project collaborations!
